# Surface brief: the map (`site/index.html`)

- **Scope:** the whole map surface. On 2026-09-30 it is re-skinned from the "Estampe" print world to an old-map world. Function, content and layout stay as they are.
- **Mode:** Experience (the visitor is inside the map), with Operate parts (filters, dice, place card).
- **Audience and action:** TikTok viewers on phones. They find a place, then watch its video in one tap.
- **Constraints:**
  - keep phone performance;
  - category colours and icons come from the Sheet;
  - EN/FR/JA.

## Direction contract

**THESIS.** Japan as an antique relief map: a shaded sepia model of the islands on an aged turquoise sea, lettered and ornamented by a cartographer's hand, where every TikTok place is a marked site. It rejects both the dark web-map dashboard and the flat modern hypsometric tint.

**OWN-WORLD.**
- **Map.** The sea is aged turquoise (#8fbab2), with engraved water-lines hugging the coasts and the islands casting a soft umber shadow onto it. The land is a sepia ramp (pale sand, ochre-tan, umber, bone-white peaks) under strong umber hillshade. The sky and far haze are parchment.
- **Paper.** A grain, faint foxing and a burnt-edge vignette lie over the whole sheet.
- **Interface.** Parchment plates (#efe4c8) with a double ink rule, sepia ink text, square 2px corners. Cartographer's red is used only for actions and chosen states.
- **Ornaments.** A compass rose that turns with the map and resets north, sea names in italic copperplate (IM Fell English, and Zen Antique for Japanese), and a title scroll reading Japan · 日本 · Japon bottom-left.
- **Places.** Crests in the Sheet colour aged toward sepia, with a parchment ring and an ink outline.

**STORY.** A viewer from TikTok opens an old map of Japan lying on the table. The islands stand up from the paper and the seas are named in copperplate. They tap a marked site, the camera flies down into the relief, and a parchment card opens one tap from the video.

**FIRST VIEWPORT (phone).**
- A one-line parchment header: logo, name, languages.
- Two parchment buttons: Categories and Random.
- Japan standing upright across the screen: sepia relief on a turquoise sea, places as small dots, the sea names lying on the water.
- The compass rose above the reset button at the right.
- The Japan · 日本 · Japon scroll at the bottom left.
- On first visit only, a paper hint at the bottom.

**FORM.** An antique engraved relief map, pinned by the owner through three reference images (a parchment map with a scroll and a compass rose, a shaded beige relief on a turquoise sea, an old geological relief map). The owner chose a turquoise sea, the full interface restyled as an old map, and four ornaments: scroll, compass rose, sea names, aged paper. Seed key: none. The direction was pinned by the owner, and the launcher is not installed, so concept-seed did not run.

**Signature interaction.** The compass rose turns live with the map: it spins slowly during the start-up turntable, and a tap swings north back up. The vertical cartouche on the card still unrolls as before.
