# Surface brief: the map (`site/index.html`)

- **Scope:** the whole map surface, redesigned from scratch.
- **Mode:** Experience (the visitor is inside the map), with Operate parts (filters, dice, place card).
- **Audience and action:** TikTok viewers on phones. They find a place, then watch its video in one tap.
- **Constraints:**
  - keep phone performance;
  - category colours and icons come from the Sheet;
  - EN/FR/JA.

## Direction contract

**THESIS.** The map is a woodblock print of Japan's famous places (meisho), like Hiroshige's series, and every TikTok place is one "view". It rejects the category default: a dark glass web-map dashboard with teardrop pins.

**OWN-WORLD.**
- **Map.** The sea is Prussian blue (bero-ai). The sky is a bokashi gradient from deep indigo to a pale ochre horizon. The land runs sage → ochre → brown → gofun white, with indigo hillshade.
- **Panels.** Aizome indigo cloth with near-square corners and no glass. Text is kinari paper colour, and vermilion (shu) is used only for actions.
- **Places.** Kamon-like crests: the Sheet colour is harmonised toward indigo, with a paper ring.
- **Titles.** Place titles sit on paper cartouches in sumi ink, with the Japanese name set vertically.
- **Type.** Zen Antique (Meiji letterpress) for display, Noto Sans for the UI.

**STORY.** A viewer arrives from TikTok and sees Japan as a print, dotted with the places. They tap one. The camera flies there and the card opens like a print with its title cartouche, one tap from the video.

**FIRST VIEWPORT (phone).**
- A one-line header: logo, name, languages.
- Two small indigo buttons: Categories and Random.
- Japan framed large across the screen, with the places as small coloured dots over the relief.
- The bokashi horizon at the top.
- On first visit only, a paper hint at the bottom: "Tap a place to watch its video".

**FORM.** Ukiyo-e meisho print, first on my list, over urushi lacquer and goshuin stamp book. The owner chose it among 3 directions. Seed key: none. The launcher was not installed, so concept-seed did not run (a degraded run, disclosed to the owner).

**Signature interaction.** When a place opens, its vertical Japanese-name cartouche unrolls down over the photo (clip-path, about 500 ms). With reduced motion, it fades in instead.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
