# TikTok promotion and videos: instructions

Everything about the promotion videos: motion design, Remotion, editing the owner's footage, voice-overs, rushes shot in the real map (`outils/video/`, outputs in `videos/`, git-ignored). The map features the films rely on (`?ambiance=non`, `?points=non`, the Halloween event, the dive and its MP4 recording…) are described in `instructions/carte.md`; the rules shared by every subject are in `CLAUDE.md`. Split out of `CLAUDE.md` on 2026-10-26.

## Commands

- `python outils/video/tourner.py`: shoots the 30 s promo film (9:16) in the real map and edits it with music into `videos/` (git-ignored). `--film voyage|zoom|pov` shoots one of the three other films. See Promo film. `--brouillon` makes a quick draft. Shoot one film at a time: two at once both timed out waiting for the map.

## The videos

### Promo film (`outils/video/`, 2026-10-09)

The owner asked for a motion design video of the map: 9:16, 30 s at most, in the site's style, showing the main features, with a call to action at the end. It is shot in the real site, not drawn: `tourner.py` opens the local site in headless Edge with the real GPU, at a phone size (432 × 768 CSS px, device scale 2.5, `map.setPixelRatio(2.5)`, so 1080 × 1920 and the interface at a real phone's size).
- **Slow motion (`ralenti.js`, an init script).** After `__ralentir()`, the page's time runs 10× slower (`performance.now`, `Date.now`, `requestAnimationFrame`, timers, CSS animations via `playbackRate`). Flights are smooth and tiles arrive in time. A CDP `Page.captureScreenshot` (JPEG, `clip.scale` 2.5, about 70 ms) is taken each time the film clock passes 1/30 s. A draft runs at ×4 and 15 frames/s.
- **The direction (`realisation.js`, injected after load).** A film clock that stops during pauses: under a paper wipe, the map jumps to the next view and waits for its tiles (`areTilesLoaded`, at most 5 s), and those moments are not filmed. Cues at film times trigger real actions: `flyTo`, the `#hash` deep link (card), clicks on the dice, favorites, "Draw the route", a legend, the music button; `jouerTyphon()`, `jouerScene('baleine')`, `faireVoler()`. The overlay (`#film`, classes prefixed `fm-`: the site already has `.cartouche`, `.logo`…) draws titles as parchment cartouches with the double rule (Zen Antique, red words), ink taps, torn-paper wipes with a red seal (旅, 伝, 海), and the end card. It is laid out for a 540 px wide screen and scaled with `zoom`.
- **Scenes** (8 × 3.75 s = 2 bars of "Michikusa" by PeriTune, 128 BPM, its first beat at 0.31 s): the whole map with the hook title; a dive onto Himeji Castle in 3D; its card and the heart; the dice (Kyoto, Temples, `Math.random` pinned so it lands on Kinkaku-ji); favorites and their route; the Orochi legend found, with cranes; the living sea (the typhoon is panned to the middle of the screen, the whale put in open sea) and the music panel; the call to action (logo, "Explore Japan in 3D, it's free", the address, "Link in bio", EN · FR · 日本語, the music credit). Titles stay out of TikTok's caption zone and right-hand buttons. The game and developer mode are never shown.
- **Output.** `promo-carte.mp4` (H.264 CRF 16, music at −14 LUFS, fading out over the last 1.3 s) and `promo-carte-sans-musique.mp4`, for a TikTok sound. The music is CC BY: credit "Michikusa · PeriTune (CC BY 4.0)" is on the end card; put it in the caption too.
- In English, the language of the account's videos. A French version means translating the titles in `realisation.js` and setting `langue` to `fr` in `tourner.py`.

**Three more films (2026-10-10).** The owner asked for a different motion design; from three concepts they said "make all 3". `tourner.py --film <name>` picks one in `FILMS`: its scripts, music (a CC BY track of `site/musique/`, credited on its end card), where the track starts, output name (`videos/film-<name>.mp4` and `-sans-musique.mp4`) and the visitor's favorites. They share `commun.js` (`window.__creerFilm({ duree, css })`: the film clock with its pauses, `quand`, `sansFilmer` (pause the clock while the map changes: a cut), `chaqueImage`, `toucher` (ink drop or a phone screen-recording dot), `flash`, `exposer({ preparer })`); `realisation.js` keeps its own copy. The music was picked by measuring tempo and loudness (`numpy` on the decoded track): 118 bpm for Avenue Cafe, Awayuki's big entrance at exactly 20.0 s.
- **The places' own videos (2026-10-10).** The owner asked to see each place's video in the voyage and POV films, as the guided tour shows it. TikTok's player does not play in an automated browser, and we must not get around that, so the films use the owner's own video files: `VIDEOS_LIEUX` in `tourner.py` (default `D:\Montage\Vidéo lieux random japon`, one finished TikTok edit per place, named after it; env var `VIDEOS_LIEUX` to change it). `EXTRAITS` maps a place's TikTok video id to (file, start in s), picked on contact sheets (a strong shot, after the dive and the title overlay). `preparer_extraits` cuts 4 s into `videos/extraits/<id>-<start>.mp4` (720 × 1280, no sound, a keyframe every 0.5 s), only the missing ones. While shooting, Playwright routes serve them at `/__extrait/<id>.mp4`, and `https://www.tiktok.com/player/**` gets `faux_lecteur`: a page that plays the clip slowed like the page (`playbackRate` = the slow-motion factor) and speaks the player's postMessage API (`onPlayerReady`, `onStateChange`, `onCurrentTime`; `play`, `pause`, `seekTo`: a seek restarts the clip and offsets the reported time, so `visite.js` sees its start reached). The file named "Daibutsu-den NARA" shows the Echizen Daibutsu in Fukui (its own title says so), so Tōdai-ji was left out.
- **`voyage.js`, "The great journey"** (Ametsuchi, PeriTune): one take, no interface. The camera flies north to south, high between stops and low and slow over each: Hirosaki Castle, Fuji Shibazakura (the Sheet place at the foot of Mount Fuji; the camera passes between the two), Kinkaku-ji, Itsukushima, Sakurajima, Shuri Castle. At each stop a red ink dot pulses on the place and a photo-print card (`.fm-carte`) opens beside it, left or right (`cote`; on the right it sits higher, above TikTok's buttons), with the place's clip and its name in IM Fell plus its Japanese name, tied to the dot by an ink thread (an SVG line from the card's nearest edge). The clips are loaded as blobs in `preparer()` and start just before their card opens, at the slow-motion rate (`window.__facteur`). "From Hokkaidō…", "…to Okinawa", then it pulls back and turns to the whole of Japan (the site's phone start view) under a map-title end card with the address on a red ribbon. The camera is `jumpTo` every frame along a precomputed track: keys `[t, lng, lat, zoom, pitch]` interpolated by monotone cubics in Mercator, the bearing following the direction of flight (smoothed, then turning to 38°). **The centre's height is frozen** (`map.setCenterClampedToGround(false)` + `jumpTo({ elevation })`): otherwise it follows the ground under the centre and the camera jumps over every mountain. `preparer()` flies the track once (every 0.2 s, waiting for tiles) to fill the cache and sample the ground's height, then smooths it (σ 0.6 s). Pins, region names, legends, the watermark and the title scroll are hidden (`display: none`; `visibility` let the region names' inner spans show).
- **`zoom.js`, "The zoom out"** (Awayuki, PeriTune, from 13.0 s so its rise lands at 7 s; its bars last 1.41 s): it opens close on Itsukushima's 3D torii, orbiting slowly, under a "1 place" counter plate and "I filmed in Japan…". The owner found the first version badly shot (2026-10-10): the pins popped at the frame's edges while the map turned, as 11 px dots. The pull-back now goes by steps of two bars (A to E): a quick move, then an almost still hold (two near-identical keys of `CLES` `[t, lng, lat, zoom, pitch, bearing]`, monotone cubics, so the camera does not turn during holds). The bearing turns to follow Japan's axis (68° over western Honshū, down to 38° for the whole of Japan) only during moves. Holds: Hiroshima Bay (zoom 8.6), western Honshū and Shikoku (7.4), Kyūshū to Kansai (6.3, still above the 6.2 dot threshold, so the pins show their icons), half of Japan (5.3), all of Japan (4.2). Every pin is hidden (`.repere:not(.fm-vu)`) until its head (`.repere-tete`: the marker element itself has no size) is well inside the frame (5% margin); waiting pins light up nearest to the centre first, at most `RYTHME` (42) per second, so each hold gets a cascade; each pops (`scale` animation, which composes with the pins' own `transform`) with a ring in its category colour. Pins keep `--t: 1` and far-view dots are 16 px. The counter plate and texts sit top-left (over the Sea of Japan at that bearing). The total is read from the Categories badge (`#compteur`): "132 places / One map."; when the address pill arrives at the bottom, the camera eases back so Kyūshū clears it. `window.__zoomCamera(t)` gives the camera at film time `t`, for tuning views.
- **`pov.js`, "POV traveller"** (Avenue Cafe, 魔王魂): a TikTok-style video, everything timed on beats (`b(n)`). White-on-black captions ("POV: you plan your Japan trip in 30 seconds"), then 1. favorites: the Categories search typed letter by letter, the Kinkaku-ji result, the heart; Himeji Castle and Kumano Nachi-taisha come by jump cuts (`location.hash` under `sansFilmer`, with a white flash) and a heart each, counted on a chip; 2. the favorites panel and "Draw the route" (the real OSRM route and times); 3. "Guided tour of my favorites" with each place's video (the fake player above) and a 3 s stay (an option added to `#visite-duree` for the film), "next" tapped to skip the overview. Jump cuts keep it short: the first flight is seen leaving, then the clock pauses until the place's video plays, and again each time the video frame closes (the frame keeps `joue` while closing: wait for `.visite-video.visible.joue`). Under each video the place's 3D model and the red route show. The step-3 caption sits under the video frame during the tour. The end card comes over the tour's closing overview.

### Halloween videos (`outils/video/halloween/`, 2026-10-21)

The four motion-design films above did badly on TikTok (about 270 and 300 views; the owner showed two). Asked which skills or tools would make better promo videos, I advised that the format mattered more than the tooling (a real place in the first second, the map as the reveal, shorter, a TikTok sound, topical), and installed the **Remotion** and **video-shotcraft** skills (see the session memory). The owner then asked for three **16:9** videos of at most **20 s** about the Halloween event, attractive from the first second and dynamic: the first on Japan's most haunted places, the other two my own ideas. They also allowed the **Flora** connector (their account) for voice-overs.
- **The pipeline**: `rushes.py` shoots map "rushes" in the real site (night, the whole event on), `preparer.py` gathers the owner's clips, music and sound effects, `voix.py` fetches the voice-overs, `montage/` (a Remotion project, `npm install` once) edits them, `finir.py` renders each video with and without music. Everything generated goes to `videos/halloween/` (git-ignored); `videos/halloween/public` is Remotion's public dir (`remotion.config.ts`).
- **Rushes** (`rushes.py` + `plans.js`): like `tourner.py`, headless Edge with the real GPU, `ralenti.js` (×0.1) and one CDP capture each time the plan's clock passes 1/30 s, but in 16:9: a 1280 × 720 page at ×1.5 (1920 × 1080), the close shots on a 960 × 540 page at ×2 (the yōkai bigger), the cobweb/cat shot at ×3 to crop. **Not above ×2 for the map**: `qualite.js` caps its pixel ratio at 2, so a ×6 page gave a blurry map and holes in the foreground relief. The quality level is forced to fine (`localStorage.qualite`), `Math.random` is seeded (the same parade path, bats and hitodama every time), Halloween is cut for the day shot by routing `config.js` to `public: false`. A plan is a duration, camera keys `[t, lng, lat, zoom, pitch, bearing]` (monotone cubics, frozen and smoothed centre height as in `voyage.js`, scouted first) and actions at times. The plans add `body.en-visite` so the parade never starts on its own (`hyakki.js` skips tours), with CSS undoing its other effects; the season's particles stay visible while the camera moves (`.saison.repos` overridden). The parade is launched from the Halloween panel's button; `suivre` makes the camera follow it (the procession's middle, or one member after another `[[t, rank], …]`, smoothed in plan time: in slow motion the page draws far more frames per film second), after `avance` real seconds of walking. With `positions`, each member's box per frame is saved to `rushes/<plan>.json` so the edit can frame each yōkai.
- **The edit** (`montage/src`, React, `@remotion/media`, Google Fonts Zen Antique / IM Fell English / Noto Sans): shared parts in `commun.tsx` (a rush with an animated crop, the owner's clip full-frame or as a photo card, the numbered label, titles, lightning, vignette and grain, the map's sources always shown on map shots, the end card with the address ribbon and "link in bio", sound effects, music with ducking under the narrator). Cuts land on the music's beats (tempo measured with numpy: spectral-flux autocorrelation and the first attacks).
  1. `HauntedPlaces` (20 s, Kengeki · PeriTune): the Meiji Utsunoya tunnel and the title from the first frame, then Nagoro (the scarecrow classroom), Mount Osore (the jizō and pinwheels; the yūrei of the hunt on the map), Hell Valley (steam, a daytime shot slowed to 0.4: the clip's night shots carry the owner's "HOKKAIDO" title), Himeji Castle (its silhouette; Okiku on the map): each a night dive on the map with the owner's clip in a photo card; the end: all on the map, 7 yōkai to find. The opening lightning flashes from frame 4, not 0: a white first frame made a washed-out grey thumbnail.
  2. `NightParade` (17.6 s, 民族09「揺れる提灯」· 魔王魂): the lantern ghost in close-up ("Every night until Halloween… this walks across my map"), the whole procession with a 百鬼夜行 stamp, seven close-ups on the beat with a red seal and the yōkai's name (cropped from `v2-proche`, where the camera follows one member after another, by the saved boxes, 1150 px wide, with a radial spotlight on the named one: a first, wider crop showed several yōkai and you could not tell which was named), the legend, the end.   3. `SpookyMap` (16.9 s, 月と狼 · 魔王魂, taken from 21.2 s so its entry lands on the lightning at 1.6 s): the day map with its cranes, then the same camera path at night (a match cut): bats, hats; the hats, the jorō spider, the cat, the parade; the hunt (six found, Enma tapped: the bubble, "Hunt complete!", the seven drawings of `halloween-dessins.js` counted 6/7 → 7/7); the end.

Videos 1 and 2 are narrated; video 3 rides its music.

### The funnel video (`outils/video/tunnel/`, 2026-10-21)

The owner pasted a brief: a 30 s vertical 3D UI animation (1080 × 1920, **60 fps**) of the conversion funnel: a TikTok post plays → tap on the account name → the profile → tap on the bio link → the camera dives *through* the link into the mobile map → three features → outro (logo in a white disc, a statement, an italic line word by word from a blur at 27.5 s). Interface on separate 3D layers with depth and soft shadows, exponential and spring curves (no linear or soft moves, no fade to black), depth of field, motion blur, Flora voice and sound design. The owner sent their profile and the Aogashima post **as seen from another account, in English** (TikTok blocks bots; never fetch TikTok pages), picked Aogashima or Etajima-so (I took Aogashima: it is on the map, so its card can close the loop), validated the "nervous" spring and asked for a slightly faster dive; I wrote the voice text.
- **Curves** (`montage/src/theme.ts`): a damped spring solved analytically, x(t) = 1 − e^(−ζωt)(cos ω_d t + ζω/ω_d sin ω_d t), stiffness 380, damping 18 (20 % overshoot, settled in 0.45 s), for every element that arrives (also sampled into a CSS `linear()` easing for the map pins, in the page itself); exponential S curves for slides and whip pans; the dive multiplies the zoom: ×0.96 (a 0.3 s pull-back) then × 50^E(u), E(u) = (e^(4u) − 1)/(e^4 − 1), over 0.85 s.
- **The TikTok part** (`TikTok.tsx`, `Telephone.tsx`): the post and the profile are rebuilt in HTML from the screenshots (`videos/tunnel/sources/`, not in git) at 1080 × 2340 (the screenshot × 1.17), TikTok Sans and Noto Color Emoji, icons redrawn as SVG (`Icones.tsx`); only the profile grid thumbnails are crops of the screenshot. As a visitor who does not follow yet: a red "Follow" button; the sound disc shows the account's photo (the real post shows another artist's cover). Each element is a `Calque` at its own `translateZ` (the pages are never clipped: `overflow` or `clip-path` would flatten the 3D). One continuous camera (perspective 2600 px): it looks at the post, pushes to the name, the pages slide (post out left, profile in from the right), it floats, goes to the link, pulls back and dives; the layers flatten (`kz` → 0) before the dive, since a `translateZ` scaled ×50 would pass the camera. The link's text stays readable up to about ×5, then its cyan pill turns white and fills the screen, which then opens on the map through a growing rounded rectangle. The background is the post's video, blurred and darkened.
- **The map** (`rushes.py` + `plans.js` on the Halloween shooting engine, now at 60 fps: `tourner(..., ips=60)`): three takes on a 540 × 960 phone page at ×2, day, no Halloween (the video must outlive 31 October): `tn-japon` (all Japan and its dots, which fade while the camera rushes to central Honshū, then the pins pop from the centre outwards with the spring), `tn-aogashima` (a dive onto the island, then an orbit), `tn-filtres` (west Japan: every pin shrinks away at "Hide all", the shrines pop back, then all pins return as the camera pulls back to Japan). Pins pop with Web Animations on `.repere-tete` (`scale`/`translate` individual properties, origin at the tip); animations on pins that the filter takes off the page must be cancelled by reference (they are no longer in `document`) or they keep their last, invisible frame when they come back. Each take's actions are timed to the edit (`temps.ts`).
- **Interface stills** (`captures.py`): the phone site on a 432 × 768 page at ×5 with the map hidden and a transparent page (`omit_background`): each button cut out with its shadow, Aogashima's card, and the categories menu in three states (all, none, shrines only) with its rows' boxes; the mouse is moved off the page first (a hovered row stays highlighted).
- **The edit** (`Carte.tsx`): the takes on a 3D plane that sways, lands with the spring and whips from one take to the next; the buttons pop on their own layers above it; a tilt-shift (`backdrop-filter` blur masked to the top and bottom of the frame) that blurs everything behind the card, which unfolds from a bottom hinge facing the camera; the menu falls in slices (its header and four rows; the map stays visible below, where the pins change); a finger with a ripple for each tap; short labels for viewers without sound; the map credits at the bottom. Motion blur (`@remotion/motion-blur`, `CameraMotionBlur`, 8 samples, 14 during the dive) only in the fast windows.
- **Sound** (`voix.py`, `Son.tsx`): nine lines by "Sarah" (ElevenLabs v3 through Flora), first person avoided ("every place, one link", "a place from the videos"); four effects made with Flora (tap, dive whoosh with its impact at 0.45 s, card pop, a cascade of pops for the pins), the others reused from earlier videos (`preparer.py`); the music "Amenoshita3" (PeriTune, CC BY 4.0, from 30 s) halved under the voice. About $1.67 of the owner's Flora balance.
- **Rendering pitfalls.** Use `OffthreadVideo` (Remotion's compositor), not `@remotion/media`'s `Video`: under the motion blur's many `Freeze` copies, the latter silently drew a black map for 8 s of a full render (short ranges were fine). The compositor in turn stopped with "Output changed" when the dive's 14 samples all asked for the post's clip: the clip is re-encoded with explicit colour tags and a keyframe every 15 frames, and the phone's blurred background uses a small pre-blurred copy (`aogashima-flou.mp4`) that is no longer drawn once the profile covers the screen (7.4 s). With the default concurrency (one tab per two cores, 10 here) the compositor ran out of memory: `finir.py` renders with `--concurrency=4` and a 2 GB video cache. Every clip and take must last until the last frame that shows it, motion-blur samples included (one frame ahead).
- **Output** (`finir.py`): `videos/tunnel/carte-japon-tunnel-tiktok.mp4` and `-sans-musique.mp4`, −14 LUFS (the music about 8.5 dB under the voice while she speaks), BT.709 TV range, 60 fps.

### The 3D walk videos (`outils/video/balade/`, 2026-10-26)

The owner, about to make the 3D walk public, asked for three TikTok motion designs (9:16) presenting it, « dans le même style que le format tunnel », very dynamic, in the art direction of the map and the walk, with a Flora voice and sounds, to make people want to come and play: one on the whole walk (all places in a row), one on a single walk in detail (secrets…), the third my choice. Made with the **video-shotcraft** skill in its autonomous mode (no independent review agent: the owner's rule is no subagents unless asked) and the Remotion skills. 30 s, 31 s and 32.4 s (each composition has its own `LONGUEUR`), 1080 × 1920, 60 fps, English.
- **The three films.** `BaladeTour` (« I turned the places from my videos… into a video game »: each of the five places first in the owner's own clip, then a slanted light line wipes it away and the same place appears in the game, tags « REAL » / « IN MY GAME »; the companion choice; one game moment per two beats: the wild deer of the tall grass, a stamp, the koi, night, fireworks, the cave's light; the map dives on Itsukushima, its card slides up and a finger taps « Visit in 3D », a portal opens into the walk; the end card). `BaladeTakachiho` (the real Manai falls wiped into the game's; the boat under the falls and its stamp, the flowing noodles, the night kagura, the glint on the cliff, the mirror on the sakaki, the eclipse, the door of the cave and its golden rays, the koban, the magatama necklace). `BaladeSecrets` (the four secrets — the sacred crows and the bamboo door, the treasure map and the dig, the Heike firefly, Okiku's plate — a koban counter that climbs, the fifth teased with the mirror's glint; Bunbuku's shop; armour, fireworks, the secret companions, the seasons).
- **Shooting the walk** (`rushes.py` + `plans.js`): the real walk (`site/balade/`) in headless Edge with the real GPU, a 540 × 960 page at ×2, `ralenti.js` (×0.08) and one CDP capture each time the take's clock passes 1/60 s; `Math.random` seeded. The HUD for video: no title, buttons, joystick or help line; the dialogue box lifted above TikTok's caption (`--bas: 240px`) and narrowed away from its right-hand buttons. A take is a scenario in page time (`plans.js`, `window.__PRISES`): `preparer` (place the traveller and companion, night at once, the bag: `jeu.donner('manju' | 'miroir' | 'assiette' | 'fu')`, `jeu.voirIndice`), `quand` (timed actions), `chaque` (every frame: a camera path `o.chemin` of keys [t, from, to, fov] set as `M.cadre`, or the game's own cameras; `o.lire` reads dialogues by itself, choosing an option; `o.agir(id)` runs an interaction as if A was pressed; `jeu.marcher` walks a path for the long secrets). Page-level settings (place, companion, `localStorage`: stamps, secrets, purchases) are in `PRISES` of `rushes.py`. `garder` films only some passages of a long take ([start, end] in take seconds): between them the page runs at normal speed (`window.__changerFacteur`, added to `ralenti.js`), one file per passage (`<take>-1.mp4`…, their starts in `rushes/index.json`). Changing speed mid-take needs `window.__minuteriesVirtuelles` (set by `plans.js`; also added to `ralenti.js`): timers are then kept in page time and fired from the animation loop. Without it a timer started at normal speed fired 12× too early once slowed (dialogue text typed instantly) and one started slowed fired 12× too late at normal speed (the eclipse came 8 s late). `--brouillon` makes a quick draft (×0.5, 15 fps) and a contact sheet. Things learnt: the noodle game needs A when a bundle is within 0.5 m of the seat (the take watches the bundles' meshes); rowing to the falls works from the east (from the south a moored boat stops the boat 6 m short); the trio's choice needs the « Three companions… » page validated first; the wardrobe can be driven with its panel hidden (`.garde-robe { display: none }`) and a frontal camera.
- **The map part** (`carte.py`, `carte.js`): a dive on Itsukushima with the Halloween shooting engine (`halloween/rushes.py`, port 8123), and the place card captured at ×4 with the dev-only « Visit in 3D » button forced visible (`#fiche a.dev`; the card is now `#fiche.ouverte`, scrolled to its buttons).
- **Sound, all original or already licensed**: the music is the walk's own tunes rendered offline (`musique.py`: `musique.js` driven in an `OfflineAudioContext` whose clock the script advances; the intro's first beat at 0.15 s exactly; each video starts on the melody's entrance, beat 8: odori 112 BPM, Takachiho 92, Aogashima 126), no credit needed; the game's sounds (`sons.py`: `son.js` notes through a fake `AudioContext`, and `fabriquer()` of `bruitage-calcul.js`: stamp, gift, koban, magic, companions' cries, puff, rooster, rumble, fireworks…); whooshes, impacts and taps reused from the promo30 and tunnel folders (`preparer.py`); the voice (`voix.py`): Flora, ElevenLabs **v4** (about $0.05 a line, v3 is $0.12), « Sarah », 21 lines, about $1.0 of the owner's balance (≈ $1 left after).

Text is the walk's dialogue box (« cadre »: cream paper, 3 px ink, red inner line, M PLUS Rounded 1c 800, red words between `*`), place labels like the walk's title (a red disc with the number); Japanese names in Windows fonts (M PLUS Rounded's Japanese comes in 118 slices). Shots and captions are in beats (`b(n)`).
- **The voice says what the text shows, when it shows it** (the owner's feedback on 2026-10-26: in videos 2 and 3 some lines overlapped, and the voice sometimes said something other than the caption on screen). A whole Flora line was often longer than its shot (« But look closely. Something shines on the cliff. » 3.4 s on a 2.6 s shot, « Each secret gives you a gold koban. Spend them at Bunbuku's shop! » 4.2 s on 2.9 s), so it ran over the next line and under the next captions; the last line of video 2 was even cut by the end. Now: `voix.py` cuts the long lines at their pauses (`COUPES`: the seconds measured on `voix/court`, a 10 ms fade at each cut; no new Flora generation), each piece is placed on the shot and caption it speaks, and each caption has the voice's words (« Row under the *waterfall* », not « falls »; the rewards in the order the voice lists them). `Son.tsx` stops the render if two lines overlap (less than 0.08 s apart), if a line has no duration in `durees`, or if it ends less than 0.5 s before the end. A caption may stay silent (the koi, night, seasons), but a voiced caption appears within 0.1 s of its words. To check: `python outils/video/balade/voix.py mesurer b5 c7` gives each line's speech spans; a frame at the middle of each piece must show its caption.
